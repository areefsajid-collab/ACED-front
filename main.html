About ACED*

Hi! I'm Areef Sajid, a Computer Science student at IBA. I created ACED* because I believe education is a basic right, not a luxury.

Too many brilliant kids in Pakistan can't access quality learning resources. That stops here. Whether you go to school or not, you deserve good materials.

ACED* is free forever.
